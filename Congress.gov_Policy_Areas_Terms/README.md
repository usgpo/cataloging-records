# Records with Congress.gov Policy Areas Terms

In October 2022, Metadata and Collection Services started to assign terms from the Congressional Research Service’s (CRS) [Congress.gov Policy Areas (CGPA) terms list](https://www.congress.gov/help/field-values/policy-area), which CRS uses for bills and resolutions on [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/), in Catalog of U.S. Government Publications (CGP) records for congressional publications. There are 32 terms in the CRS list. The CGPA terms provide access to a high-level facet to the content of the publications. The terms are entered in MARC 650 fields and are identified by the MARC Subject Heading and Term Source code cgpa in subfield $2.

The file contains 23,310 records that include at least one of the CGPA terms. A sample set of records may be retrieved by this CGP expert mode search: [wlts=PUBPOLTERMS](https://catalog.gpo.gov/F/?func=find-c&ccl_term=wlts%3Dpubpolterms&x=0&y=0).
